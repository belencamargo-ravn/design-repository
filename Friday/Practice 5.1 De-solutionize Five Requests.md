# Practice 5.1: De-solutionize Five Requests

Five feature requests, rewritten as problem statements that describe the user, the friction and the consequence, without jumping straight to a solution.

---

## 1. "Add a tipping button"

**Problem Statement**: Requesters can't find a way to tip a provider because there is no such option in the app. This leads to a growing number of people feeling frustrated and avoiding this transaction, so Vello isn't capturing this flow and is losing user engagement.

---

## 2. "Make the search bar bigger"

**Problem Statement**: Requesters with urgent household needs struggle to quickly locate a specific service on the Home screen because the search bar is too small and doesn't let them type easily. This leads users to drop the search and fail to find the help they need.

---

## 3. "Add push notifications for new requests"

**Problem Statement**: Providers miss new job opportunities while they aren't using the app, because there is no notification system alerting them to new requests. This leads to lost income for providers and slower response times, which frustrates requesters.

---

## 4. "Let admins bulk-approve providers"

**Problem Statement**: Admins experience severe time fatigue during moderation sessions due to tedious, repetitive credential checks, having to go through each one individually even when they're near-identical. This leads to a frustrating, time-consuming experience for admins and delays provider launch times.

---

## 5. "Add a dark mode"

**Problem Statement**: Admins reviewing moderation queues during late-night hours experience harsh screen glare and eye strain, which reduces focus and increases the likelihood of moderation mistakes.
