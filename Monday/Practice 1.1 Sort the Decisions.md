# Practice 1.1: Sort the Decisions (Vello)

## 1. Classified Decisions List

1. **Vello charges providers a flat monthly fee instead of per-booking commission.**
   * **Category**: Product
   * **Ownership**: PM

2. **The "Book now" button is persimmon, not forest green.**
   * **Category**: Visual
   * **Ownership**: Design

3. **A requester must verify their address before they can post a need.**
   * **Category**: UX
   * **Ownership**: Design

4. **Provider availability shows as a weekly calendar, not a list.**
   * **Category**: UI
   * **Ownership**: Design

5. **Vello launches with dog-walking only, then expands.**
   * **Category**: Product
   * **Ownership**: PM

6. **Rating stars use the amber color from the design system.**
   * **Category**: Visual
   * **Ownership**: Design

7. **After booking, the user lands on a confirmation screen, not back on the feed.**
   * **Category**: UX
   * **Ownership**: Design

8. **The admin verification queue sorts oldest-first.**
   * **Category**: UX
   * **Ownership**: Design

9. **Body text is set in Hanken Grotesk at 17px.**
   * **Category**: Visual 
   * **Ownership**: Design

10. **Vello requires a real-name profile, not a username.**
    * **Category**: Product
    * **Ownership**: PM
    
---

## 2. Reframing & Ownership Summary

* **Decisions I Own (3/10)**: Decisions #1, #5 and #10 directly affect business strategy, operational priority, and core product policy.
* **Decisions Handed to Design as Problems (7/10)**: Decisions #2, #3, #4, #6, #7, #8 and #9. Rather than specifying UI components, I frame these as user problems so design can explore the best visual and structural solutions.
