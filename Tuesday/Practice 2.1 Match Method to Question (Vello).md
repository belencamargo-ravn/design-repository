# Practice 2.1: Match Method to Question (Vello)

## 1. Categorization & Unblocked Decisions

1. **Why do requesters drop off before completing a booking?**
   * **Type**: Qualitative + Quantitative
   * **Method**: Field Studies & Contextual Inquiry or Open-Ended Surveys & In-App Feedback
   * **Decision Unblocked**: Should engineering redesign the booking UI flow (if drop-off is caused by UX confusion) OR should product adjust our pricing transparency (if drop-off is caused by hidden fees/price shock)?

2. **What percentage of providers respond within an hour?**
   * **Type**: Quantitative
   * **Method**: Analytics & Web Metrics
   * **Decision Unblocked**: If the figure is too low, should we consider another solution to facilitate and expedite the submission of responses by suppliers, or perhaps incorporate automated responses to maintain user interest?

3. **Can a first-time admin figure out how to approve a provider?**
   * **Type**: Qualitative
   * **Method**: Usability Testing
   * **Decision Unblocked**: If a user cannot easily accomplish this (or cannot do it at all) should the design team redesign the entire flow to make it more intuitive and easier to understand?

4. **What does "trust" actually mean to a Vello requester?**
   * **Type**: Qualitative
   * **Method**: User Interviews (1-on-1)
   * **Decision Unblocked**: Should we invest in identity/background-check verification badges as the primary trust signal OR should we build a peer review and rating system as the primary trust signal?

5. **Which neighborhoods have the most unmet demand?**
   * **Type**: Qualitative
   * **Method**: Field Studies & Contextual Inquiry
   * **Decision Unblocked**: Should we launch in the new neighborhoods showing high unmet demand OR should we hold off on expansion and focus marketing spend on deepening provider supply in our current neighborhoods?

6. **Is our new onboarding flow easier than the old one?**
   * **Type**: Qualitative
   * **Method**: Usability Testing
   * **Decision Unblocked**: Should we ship the new onboarding flow to all users OR should we keep the current flow live and send the new one back for further iteration?

---

## 2. Qual + Quant Combination Flag
* **Primary Combination Point**: For "Why do requesters drop off before completing a booking?", quantitative data tells us **where** and **how much** drop-off happens in the booking flow, while qualitative data explains **why** requesters are dropping off there, so both are needed to act on the finding.
