# Practice 2.2: Spot the Flawed Question (Vello)

## 1. Question Evaluation & Rewrites

### Question 1: "Would you use an app that finds local help?"
* **Flaw Type**: Hypothetical
* **Why It's Flawed**: Human self-forecasting is notoriously inaccurate; people are poor predictors of their own future behavior, especially toward a product that doesn't exist yet.
* **Rewritten Question**: Have you ever used an app to find local help? Where do you usually go when you need help around the house?

### Question 2: "Tell me about the last time you needed help around the house."
* **Flaw Type**: No flaw
* **Why It's Flawed**: This question is well-formed: it's behavioral, specific, and open-ended, so it surfaces what the person actually did rather than what they think they'd do.
* **Rewritten Question**: No rewrite needed.

### Question 3: "Don't you think trust is important?"
* **Flaw Type**: Yes/No, Leading
* **Why It's Flawed**: It's a closed question that can be answered with a single word, and it's phrased so that disagreeing feels awkward. It nudges the respondent toward a predetermined answer instead of surfacing their own view.
* **Rewritten Question**: Tell me about a previous experience with letting a stranger into your home.

### Question 4: "How do you find and pay providers today?"
* **Flaw Type**: Double-barreled
* **Why It's Flawed**: It packs two separate workflows into one question. Finding a provider and paying one can involve very different tools, habits, and pain points, so a single answer muddies both.
* **Rewritten Question(s)**:
  - How do you currently find providers for jobs like this?
  - Once you've picked someone, how do you handle payment?

### Question 5: "What frustrates you about TaskRabbit?"
* **Flaw Type**: Leading
* **Why It's Flawed**: It assumes both that the person uses TaskRabbit and that the experience was frustrating, which may not be true.
* **Rewritten Question**: What has your experience using TaskRabbit been like? Walk me through how you typically use it.

### Question 6: "Would you pay more for a verified neighbor?"
* **Flaw Type**: Hypothetical
* **Why It's Flawed**: Human self-forecasting is notoriously inaccurate, especially when money is involved; people tend to overstate what they'd actually pay.
* **Rewritten Question**: Tell me about the last time you hired a neighbor for help. How did that come about?
